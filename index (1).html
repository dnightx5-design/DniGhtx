Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Open Services, tap Order Now, answer the questions in the chat, review your summary and submit.

How much does it cost?

Each service shows its starting price in NPR. We confirm your order after reviewing your answers.

How do I pay?

We accept eSewa only. After we confirm your order, pay a 50% advance by scanning our eSewa QR (shown after you submit and on the Contact page), then send the payment screenshot to us. The rest is paid on delivery.

How do I get my design?

Designs are digital files that are sent to you online.

How long does it take?

You choose your deadline in the order chat, and we confirm it when we review your order.

How do I check my order?

Go to Contact, enter your order number and the phone or email you used, and tap Check status.

Terms and privacy

Payment

Payment is by eSewa only. After we confirm your order, you pay a 50% advance by scanning our QR and sending us the payment screenshot. Work starts after the advance is received. The remaining 50% is paid on delivery.

Changes and refunds

Changes and refunds are discussed and agreed with you before work begins.

Your information

We store your name, contact details and order answers only to process your order. We do not sell or share them.
